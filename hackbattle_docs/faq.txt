FAQ
Q: What is HackBattle?
A: HackBattle, organized by IEEE CS, is a dynamic 36-hour hackathon that goes beyond coding. It is a journey of teamwork, mentorship, and innovation where participants tackle real-world problems, attend workshops and tech talks, and network with industry leaders.

Q: How do I register?
A: Registration is through the official graVITas website. Teams of 3–5 members must be finalized before the event. Solo participation is not allowed.

Q: Is HackBattle fresher-friendly?
A: Yes. HackBattle is designed to support beginners as well as experienced developers, with mentorship, speaker sessions, and guidance.

Q: What should I bring?
A: Bring your laptop, charger, extension cord, and any hardware your project requires. Hardware will not be provided at the venue.

Q: Is there a team size limit?
A: Yes. Teams must have 3–5 members. Solo participation is not permitted.
