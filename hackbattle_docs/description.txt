Short Description
HackBattle is a 36-hour overnight hackathon where innovators, creators, and problem-solvers collaborate to turn ideas into impactful solutions. Participants build projects tackling real-world challenges while learning and networking with industry leaders, mentors, and peers. HackBattle is a legacy event at VIT, known for its fresher-friendly environment, curated problem statements, and vibrant energy. Hackbattle is held on 8am 24th Sept 2025 to 25th Sept 2025 8 pm.
Registration fee is the same for vit and external participants at Rs 200

Long Description
HackBattle, organized by IEEE Computer Society (IEEE CS) is a 36-hour hackathon designed to push the limits of creativity, technology, and teamwork. The event brings together passionate students and developers to ideate, design, and build solutions aligned with curated problem statements. Teams focus on execution rather than struggling with ideation, ensuring meaningful and impactful projects.
The hackathon includes workshops, tech talks, and panel discussions led by industry experts and mentors, covering cutting-edge technologies and career insights. With 600 participants and filler activities throughout the night HackBattle fosters an atmosphere of collaboration and relentless innovation.
On Duty (OD) will be provided to all participants. If you do not have a team you can join the HackBattle Discord server (link in the navbar) to find teammates. Both external and internal students can register through the official graVITas website: www.gravitas.vit.ac.in. Participants may leave midway for genuine reasons (quiz, assessment, health issues) after informing an organizing committee member. 
Registration fee is the same for internal and external participants at Rs 200

The event will be held on 8am 24th Sept 2025 to 25th Sept 2025 8 pm.
